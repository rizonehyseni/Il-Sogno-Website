Dining & Live Music

Is food served during performances?

Yes, our full menu is available during live performances, allowing guests to enjoy a complete dining experience while listening to music. All dishes are freshly prepared and served directly at your table.

Is there an age limit for attending live music events?

Yes, for evening live music events we require guests to be at least 18 years old. This helps us maintain a comfortable atmosphere for all attendees.

Are performances held every night?

Live music performances are scheduled on selected nights throughout the week. We recommend checking our website or social media pages for the latest event updates.

Reservations

Do I need a reservation?

While walk-ins are welcome, reservations are highly recommended on weekends and busy evenings to ensure table availability and avoid waiting times.

How can I make a reservation?

You can easily make a reservation through our online booking system or by calling us directly during business hours.

Can I cancel my reservation?

Yes, reservations can be canceled or modified up to 24 hours in advance without any additional charge.

Menu & Drinks

Do you use fresh ingredients in your dishes?

Yes, all of our dishes are prepared using fresh, high-quality ingredients sourced from trusted local and international suppliers. We focus on freshness and flavor to ensure every meal meets our standards of quality and taste.

Is the menu available online?

Yes, our complete menu is available online, where you can explore our food and drink selections before visiting us.

Do you serve cocktails?

Yes, we serve a wide selection of classic and signature cocktails, prepared by our professional bartenders using high-quality ingredients.
