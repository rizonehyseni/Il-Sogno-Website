Our Story

  • 1978

    Il Sogno was first envisioned on Amalfi’s coast, when a small team of passionate chefs and locals dreamed of a place where authentic Italian cuisine could flourish. They experimented with traditional recipes, sourcing the finest local ingredients. The foundation was laid for what would become a culinary landmark, blending heritage and creativity in every dish.

  • 2006

    After decades of dedication, Il Sogno expanded, welcoming more guests and gaining recognition for its exceptional flavors. The founders introduced innovative menus while staying true to Amalfi’s culinary traditions. The restaurant became a hub for both locals and travelers, celebrated for its warm hospitality, stunning views, and dishes that honor Italian craftsmanship.

  • 2025

    Il Sogno embraces a modern era while respecting its rich history. The kitchen blends classic Amalfi recipes with contemporary techniques, highlighting sustainability and locally sourced ingredients. This new chapter ensures every guest experiences not just a meal, but a story of passion, tradition, and unforgettable memories along the enchanting Amalfi coast.
